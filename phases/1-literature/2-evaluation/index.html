    Part 2 · Tagging precedent¶

    Goal¶

    Classify 50 abstracts to create an independent reference for evaluating a classification-only model (Jev). These are a shared-method evaluation set, not necessarily papers about your phosphatase. Afterwards, apply the evaluated workflow to your own gene. Why this evaluation matters.

    Time¶

    The abstract JSON is due 10 November, before the agent session. Spread the work across several sessions. Full-text work comes after the abstract answers are submitted and locked; its scope, format and deadline will be confirmed at supervision.

    Before you start¶

    • Download your assigned S?_items.json file.
    • Complete the workshop, then compare with the solutions when invited.
    • Keep AI and full texts out of the independent abstract task.

    Classification options¶

    Use exactly these labels. The same four fields are used for students and the classifier; no generated text, quotes, confidence ratings or other extraction fields are required.

    Field Options
    is_tagging_paper yes, no, unsure
    tag_type AID, dTag, SMASH, bromo, other, unclear
    locus_strategy endogenous, exogenous, unclear
    terminus C-term, N-term, unclear
    • yes: the study attaches a degron or another tag to a protein.
    • no: it does not. A natural degron motif or small-molecule degrader alone does not count. Skip the other three fields.
    • unsure: the abstract does not settle whether tagging occurred.
    • yes and unsure both queue a paper for later full-text reading. Complete the other three fields using only the abstract.
    • AID includes AID2 and mAID; dTag covers dTAG; SMASH covers SMASh; bromo covers BromoTag.
    • other: a stated tag outside those families, including fluorescent or epitope tags. unclear: the category cannot be established from the abstract.
    • endogenous: tagging at the native locus. exogenous: an ectopic expression construct, transfected or integrated. CRISPR alone does not prove endogenous tagging; exogenous does not require measured overexpression.
    • Use unclear when one option cannot represent the abstract, including mixed strategies, multiple tag types, or internal/mixed tag positions. Do not infer a terminus from what would make biological sense.

    Steps¶

    1. Open the annotation tool and load your assigned items file.
    2. Read the title and abstract; choose yes, no or unsure.
    3. For yes or unsure, classify tag type, locus strategy and terminus.
    4. Export after every session. Keep the downloaded JSON: browser storage is not a backup.
    5. Check that the tool reports 50/50 complete, then send the final export to your supervisor.

    Resume from a saved export¶

    In the same browser, load the same assigned items file to resume browser-saved work. On another browser, or after clearing storage:

    1. Load your original S?_items.json file.
    2. Click Restore saved annotations and choose your latest answers export.
    3. Check the set and record count, then confirm.

    Restore replaces current answers, rather than merging them. Export current work first if you need to keep it. Items absent from the backup become blank. Wrong sets and full-text records are rejected.

    The new scheme is version 3. Old backups and browser records are not silently converted: keep them, but reclassify with the new options. Old browser records remain untouched in a separate storage key.

    Done when¶

    • All 50 items have a screening decision.
    • Every yes or unsure record has all three additional classifications.
    • Every no record omits the additional fields.
    • The export reports 50/50 complete.
    • You have sent the abstract JSON by 10 November and kept a local copy.
    • Your supervisor confirms receipt and locking before you open full texts or inspect model answers.

    Full-text extraction uses a later, separate workflow. Function checks, degradation results, protein names and other detailed information are not part of this abstract classification task.

    Classifier instructions¶

    Supply the title and abstract only. Ask the classifier to select one of the exact labels listed above for each applicable field, using the same definitions. Run the three detail classifications only for yes or unsure. The runner—not the classifier—adds item ID, annotator identity, stage, timestamp and schema version. No text generation is needed. The repository file docs/phases/1-literature/reference/tagging-schema.json defines the saved format.

    If you get stuck¶

    Use unsure for unresolved screening and unclear for unsupported detail classifications. Ask your supervisor about a convention if necessary; do not look up the paper or ask AI to resolve your independent abstract answer.
