    Part 3 · Full-text analysis¶

    Goal¶

    Answer: where and how was this protein tagged, and what evidence shows how the tagged protein functions? Prepare independent records for evaluating the extraction agent. Include non-degron tags; documented functional defects count too.

    Open the full-text extraction app

    Time¶

    Practise on separate examples and receive three distinct evaluation papers on Tuesday 3 November; bring independent records to the 17 November meeting. Allow more than one reading session per paper. Find your three papers in your named subcollection under Full-text shortlist (provisional) in the FYP26 Zotero group. The practice subcollection is separate.

    For the group practice, allow 30–45 minutes minimum plus discussion. Each group works on one paper:

    • Examples — full text 1: cyclin A2 and cyclin B1
    • Examples — full text 2: HA-tagged μ-opioid receptor

    Each page links to the paper and supporting material. Save your group's draft before the discussion. Solutions will be released afterwards.

    Before you start¶

    • Obtain the paper and supplements; check the assigned target protein.
    • Keep practice and evaluation papers separate.
    • Do not use AI to interpret or check evaluation papers, even after receiving an agent account. References must be completed and locked first.

    Steps¶

    1. Open the app. Enter your name, paper identifier/title and target. Choose practice or evaluation and record missing source material.
    2. Answer Which protein was tagged? Use Add protein for another protein. Under each protein, answer Which tag was used? Use Add tag for another separately tested tagged version of that protein. Enter a combined tag such as mAID–GFP as one entry, not two tags. Select Next: methods, function and depletion below Add protein to continue. Each tag keeps its own methods and results. If the same tag is tested in different cell lines or as a separate rescue construct, add another tag entry and distinguish them in the model/methods answers.

    3. Complete the core questions:

      Area Record
      Identity Protein/isoform, organism, cell line/tissue and clone
      Tagging Exact tag components, N/C/internal position, fusion order and linker
      Construction Endogenous/exogenous expression, targeting method, allele status, donor/construct availability and edit validation
      Function before depletion Assay → comparator → result for the tagged protein; also applies to non-degrons

    4. If the construct uses a degron, complete the additional questions:

      Area Record
      Induction Degrader, dose, exposure time and required machinery/variant
      Measurement Method, comparator, normalisation and protein pool measured
      Efficiency and kinetics Reported depletion or remaining protein at each relevant dose/time; time course if measured
      Basal depletion and recovery Evidence without inducer; recovery after washout, if tested
      Function after depletion Biological assay, comparator and outcome; rescue or other specificity controls

    5. Give supported methods and results a short quote or faithful summary and exact figure/section/supplement location. Names alone are enough for the protein and tag questions; optional supporting information is just the source, exact location and a short summary. Identify earlier papers explicitly wherever they supply evidence.

    6. Export a draft after each session, including before New paper replaces the browser workspace. Use Restore JSON to continue from an exported file.
    7. Use Check & export completed record when finished. Keep your original exports for the reference-locking meeting.

    Name the traps: expression, localisation, survival or successful depletion alone does not establish normal function. An untagged allele may compensate. A phenotype after depletion does not prove the tag was harmless beforehand. Do not estimate depletion percentages from blot images; preserve qualitative claims when no number is reported.

    Done when¶

    You have three paper-level JSON exports, with each protein linked to its tags, and separate results for each tagged version, traceable evidence and explicit gaps. Use not reported when you cannot find information, not tested when the study explicitly did not test it, unclear for ambiguity, and source unavailable for inaccessible material. Explain each gap; use not applicable only where offered and appropriate.

    A completed export passes form checks: it is not automatically an adjudicated or locked reference. Preserve originals when resolving disagreements. Keep these files separate from abstract classifications; the apps and schemas differ.

    If you get stuck¶

    1. Check Methods, figure legends and supplements; record any missing sources.
    2. Export a draft and bring unresolved questions or app errors to the session. Browser saving is local to that browser and website address, not a submission.
    3. Email your supervisor if access or tooling blocks progress. Do not ask an agent to fill evidence gaps.
