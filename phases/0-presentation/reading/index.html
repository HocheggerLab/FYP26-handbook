    Reading for the presentation¶

    Starting points only. Follow what looks interesting; you are not expected to read all of this, and you are expected to find things that are not here.

    Degron tags¶
    Nabet et al. 2018, Nat Chem Biol the dTAG system — chemical degradation of a tagged protein
    Natsume et al. 2016, Cell Rep the auxin-inducible degron (AID) in human cells
    Yesbolatova et al. 2020, Nat Commun AID2 — the improved version, and why the first one leaked

    Read the figures before the text. The experiment these papers are selling is "protein gone in 30 minutes", and the figure showing that is the argument.

    AI agents¶
    Anthropic, Building effective agents what a tool-calling loop is, written plainly
    Any recent agent benchmark paper how people currently claim to measure them — and what the claims leave out

    Degraders, for contrast¶

    PROTACs and molecular glues destroy a protein without modifying the gene. Same outcome, completely different method. The difference is set out in Degrons and degraders — read that page rather than duplicating it in a talk.

    Where to search¶

    PubMed, and the papers that cite the three above. A large part of phase 1 is discovering that keyword searching alone finds a surprisingly small fraction of the relevant literature — worth noticing early.
