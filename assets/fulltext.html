Answer statuses and evidence

Answered: a supported finding, including an inconclusive experimental result. Provide a short quote or faithful summary and an exact source location.

Not reported: you checked the accessible material but could not find the information. Not tested: the study explicitly did not perform the assessment. Unclear: ambiguous or conflicting evidence. Source unavailable: you could not obtain the relevant material. Not applicable: the question does not apply.

For an unanswered status, explain why and where you checked. For cited sources, name the earlier paper and location; do not present a citation as evidence you have read. Missing supplements are not evidence that an experiment was not performed.

Local saving uses this browser and website address only. Export a draft after each session and before starting another paper. A completed export passes form checks; it is not an adjudicated or locked reference. Keep a separate copy of your original submission.
