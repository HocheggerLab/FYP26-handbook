yes: the study attaches a degron or other tag to a protein. no: it does not; a natural degron motif or small-molecule degrader alone is not a tag. unsure: the abstract does not settle this.

For no, skip the other fields. For yes or unsure, classify all three and queue the paper for later full-text reading. Do not read it before your abstract answers are submitted and locked.

AID includes AID2 and mAID; dTag covers dTAG; SMASH covers SMASh; bromo covers BromoTag. other means a stated tag outside these families, including fluorescent or epitope tags. unclear means the abstract does not identify the category.

endogenous: native-locus tagging. exogenous: an ectopic expression construct, whether transfected or integrated. CRISPR alone does not establish endogenous tagging. Select unclear if the abstract cannot support one option, including mixed strategies or multiple tag types. For the terminus, internal or mixed positions also fall under unclear in this simplified scheme.
