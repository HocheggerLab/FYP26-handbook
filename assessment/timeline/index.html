    Project schedule¶

    Meetings · Tuesdays at 1 pm

    27 October–15 December 2026. We start with the Phase 1 introduction on 27 October. Use this page for meeting preparation and deadlines.

    The route to a cloning design¶

    flowchart LR
    A["Independent references<br/>50 abstracts + 3 full texts"] --> B["Evaluate agents<br/>17–24 November"]
    B --> C["Gene-specific literature<br/>+ isoforms and structures<br/>24 November–1 December"]
    C --> D["Human cloning design<br/>1–8 December"]
    D --> E["Cloning agent comparison<br/>8–15 December"]

    The literature, isoforms and structures inform the degron design before cloning begins. The written protein introduction can wait until after Christmas; the biological reading needed for your design cannot.

    Key checkpoints¶

    • 10 November · Before the meeting

      Submit and lock your 50 abstract classifications before agent access.

    • 17 November · Bring your reference records

      Complete three independent full-text extractions. We assemble the student and supervisor data before evaluation runs.

    • 8 December · Present your design

      Present and preserve your human cloning design before the cloning-agent demonstration.

    • After Christmas · Date to be confirmed

      Submit your protein introduction: one page and one or two slides.

    Weekly meetings¶

    Tuesday, 1 pm In the meeting Work before the next meeting
    27 October Phase 1 introduction. Practise classification using the examples; try the annotation tool and check Zotero access. Begin the 50 abstracts and protein reading. Prepare the background presentation.
    3 November Background presentations. Resolve classification questions and check progress. Finish, check and export the 50 abstract classifications. Submit before the 10 November meeting.
    10 November Agent access and setup. Confirm abstract locking. Introduce the three-paper full-text reference task and its recording format. Independently extract the three assigned full-text papers, without AI or model answers. Keep these records separate from the abstract reference.
    17 November Assemble evaluation data. Combine student and supervisor records, discuss disagreements and create a separate adjudicated reference. Agree the evaluation runs and measures. Once references are locked, each student runs the evaluations and analyses the results. Preserve raw predictions and run details.
    24 November Review model performance. Decide whether the workflow is ready for gene-specific use. Receive the isoform, modification and structure data. If approved, run the workflow on your gene's literature, collect responses and verify the evidence. Inspect isoforms and structures; assemble the evidence needed for cloning.
    1 December Plan cloning from the evidence. Compare tagging precedent, isoforms and structural constraints; justify the terminus and construct strategy. Prepare your human cloning design and its rationale for presentation. Keep it independent of the cloning agent's proposed design.
    8 December Present human cloning designs. Then see the cloning agent, with and without DNAgent. Run the agent comparison. Use the agent to help develop a transparent way to quantify human–agent differences; retain your original human design and record the scoring decisions.
    15 December Summary and review. Discuss results, limitations, remaining work and the writing plan. Complete agreed follow-up analysis and work on the protein introduction and write-up after Christmas.

    What to hand in or bring¶

    Date Output
    3 November Background presentation and written work: Phase 0.
    10 November, before the meeting Fifty abstract classifications as JSON: Part 2. Keep your exported copy.
    17 November Independent full-text reference records for three papers, ready to combine with the supervisor's annotations.
    24 November Evaluation results, saved agent responses and a short account of errors and limitations.
    1 December Gene-specific tagging evidence and interpretation of the supplied isoform/structure data, ready to support cloning decisions.
    8 December Human cloning design and rationale, ready to present and preserve before agent comparison.
    15 December Progress summary, comparison results so far and unresolved questions.
    After Christmas; exact date to be confirmed Protein introduction: one page and one or two slides.

    Meeting outputs are progress checkpoints unless described as a submission or presentation. They are not additional essay assignments.

    Protect the comparisons¶

    • Independent answers first. Agent access on 10 November does not permit AI assistance on the three full-text reference papers. Complete these before seeing the extraction agent's output.
    • Keep originals. Student and supervisor records remain separate. Resolve disagreements in an adjudicated copy and lock it before the evaluation runs.
    • Check before applying. If the 24 November review finds material failures, agree a narrower or manually checked workflow rather than trusting its output.
    • Human cloning design first. Save the design presented on 8 December before inspecting the cloning agent's design. Document later revisions and agree the comparison criteria before reporting scores.

    Keep the workload bounded¶

    The full-text evaluation is three papers per student, not ten. It is a small pilot, not a comprehensive benchmark. Suitable papers can also contribute to the gene-specific analysis; do not duplicate the extraction.

    Bring your current work, one unresolved question and any blocker to each meeting. Export annotations before leaving the tool. If the schedule becomes unmanageable, agree a reduced scope with your supervisor rather than rushing the independent reference work.
